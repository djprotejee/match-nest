# MatchNest Product Notes

## Core idea

MatchNest is a personal sports calendar for followed events across Formula 1, NAVI CS2 and football.

## Main interests

- Formula 1
- NAVI CS2
- Ukraine national football team
- Barcelona

## Starred interests

- Ferrari
- UEFA Champions League
- FIFA World Cup
- UEFA Euro
- CS2 Majors
- IEM
- BLAST

## Visibility levels

- `main` - first feed, highest priority
- `starred` - important extras
- `muted` - visible, no notifications
- `hidden` - excluded
- `explore` - searchable but not followed

## Views

- Timeline - today, week, month
- Calendar - previous and future months
- Settings - follows and source preferences
- Widget - next followed event
